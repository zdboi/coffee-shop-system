# Release 1.4.2: what to do

A small update with **no database script**. It takes about 5 minutes. Any time is fine; counters don't need to stop selling.

Your current live version (1.4.1) is saved as the **last working version** (`docs/checkpoints.md`). If anything goes wrong, tell C: **"Go back to the last working version."**

## What's new
- **A QR code for your menu link** (Back Office → Settings → Menu link):
  - **Download PNG:** a large image for posts and screens.
  - **Download SVG:** for printers and design apps.
  - **Print card:** a branded "Scan for our menu" card, A6 or A5, for the counter, tables or the window.
- **A shorter menu page:** your sub-categories are now tabs at the top. People tap a tab, or swipe left and right, to move between them. The page no longer scrolls through everything at once.

## Steps

### 1. Publish the apps (2 min)
1. Open the pull request link C sends you.
2. Click **Merge pull request**, then **Confirm merge**.
3. Wait about 2 minutes for the site to update.

### 2. Reload (1 min per device)
On your computer and your phone, open the Back Office and the menu link, then reload the page twice. The counters don't change in this release, but reloading them does no harm.

### 3. Quick check (2 min)
1. Back Office → **Settings** → **Menu link**. You should see the QR code.
2. Scan it with your phone camera. Your menu should open.
3. On the menu, swipe left on the items. The next sub-category should appear and its tab should light up.
4. Optional: click **Print card**, pick A6 or A5, and print one.

## If something goes wrong
Tell C what you see (a photo of the screen helps). To undo, tell C: **"Go back to the last working version."**
